# JEI card catalyst live checks

Follow the [specification](../specs/2026-10-28-jei-card-catalysts.md) and record versions, request IDs, screenshots and results in the [validation record](../validation/2026-10-28-jei-card-catalysts.md). Use the installed default prompt with its developer override cleared.

1. Ask how to make Gold Powder using the Natural Altar. Compare the card's catalyst strip with JEI's category side panel. Hover each strip icon for its tooltip and highlight; check left-click and R for recipes, right-click and U for uses, and A twice to restore the bookmark list.
2. Ask for a torch crafting recipe and a glass smelting recipe. Confirm that crafting has no strip and smelting shows a furnace. Check recipe-slot hover, tooltip, R and U.
3. Find a category with more catalysts than fit in one column. Confirm collapsed `+N`, then hover to expand every catalyst with no `+N`, and check expanded icon tooltips and R. Record if no suitable category is found.
4. Narrow the game window until the altar card scales with its strip, then falls back to a result chip followed by distinct machine chips. Hover and use the machine chips; if there are more than three, check `+N` and its capped tooltip. Restore the window.
5. In ATM10, check Create's mixer and basin, a Mekanism or Immersive Engineering machine, and a furnace category with enough furnaces to exercise `+N` and the expanded panel.
