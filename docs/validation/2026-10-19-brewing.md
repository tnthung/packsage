## Delivered bounds and semantics

Discovery, continuations and evidence reads use the current level's brewing source. Each vanilla/custom rule table is capped at 8,192 rows; structural reads visit at most 64 container entries and 256 potion registry entries. Candidate projection is capped at 512 per call, or 4,096 with an output-item filter. Ingredients retain at most 64 declared alternatives. These limits produce partial coverage rather than a complete absence claim.

Locators include phase, row, variant and a fingerprint. An unchanged late rule is read directly, including the tested 5,000th custom rule. A moved-rule fallback scans at most 4,096 candidates; a rule outside that fallback can become unavailable. Removed or changed rules cannot replay the old record. Opaque rules are freshly readable without invoking their executable methods, and do not match an exact output-item filter.

Component preflight checks at most 16 serialized patch entries and a conservative 16,384-byte estimate for the supported simple component forms. Named potion contents and ordinary literal custom names are preserved. Other or oversized component forms return an explicit bounded marker with partial coverage; inherited default components are not mistaken for serialized changes.

Supported brewing records participate in the existing bounded planner through an independent candidate allowance. Potion components and declared output counts remain distinct, while unknown input quantities, batch/fuel/remainder constraints, overlapping rules and client authority keep the routes partial. Synthetic brewing locators are not presented as registered datapack recipe IDs.

## Acceptance limits

No Minecraft instance has been changed, installed or driven for this task. There is no installed brewing or model-behavior acceptance yet.

The supported scope is vanilla named potion/container conversions and exact declarative NeoForge `BrewingRecipe` entries. Unknown custom implementations/ingredients remain opaque. Client-loaded rules do not establish effective remote-server rules; overlapping custom rules retain precedence limits. Named potion targeting, exact shared-batch material/fuel totals and arbitrary custom mechanics are outside the existing planner's verified contract.
